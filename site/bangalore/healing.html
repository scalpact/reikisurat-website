Common questions

Do I need to book in advance?

Yes. Book online or on WhatsApp so we can keep a slot for you. In-person sessions in Bengaluru are by appointment on limited days.

How does an online (distance) session work?

You book a time, we send a Zoom link before the session, and you relax at home while the healer sends Reiki. Many people find distance healing just as calming as an in-person session.

What should I wear?

Loose, comfortable clothes. You stay fully clothed in every session.

How many sessions will I need?

Many people feel lighter after the first session. For long-standing problems we usually suggest a few sessions, and we tell you honestly after the first one.

Can I take Reiki while on medicine?

Yes. Reiki works alongside medical treatment. Never stop medicine your doctor has prescribed.
