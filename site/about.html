About us

Shree Sai Yog & Reiki Healing Centre Private Limited: a Reiki healing and training centre started in Surat in 2014 by Dr. Chirag Gajjar and Binal Gajjar, now also in Bengaluru.

Founder

Dr. Chirag Gajjar (PhD)

Reiki Grand Master · 11th generation, Usui lineage

Dr. Chirag Gajjar has practised and taught Reiki for more than 25 years. He became a Reiki Grand Master in 2005 and started the centre in Adajan, Surat in 2014 together with Binal Gajjar, a crystal and numerology expert.

Since then the centre has grown into one family of healers and students: more than 30,000 people helped and over 25,000 healing sessions given. In 2025 the centre set a world record for mass healing at the Aayush Conclave in Surat, and opened its Bengaluru branch under Dr. Varun Patel.
