## The Energy Body

Long before modern science began studying bioelectric fields, ancient Indian sages mapped the human energy body in extraordinary detail. At the centre of this system are seven primary energy centres called chakras — wheels of spinning energy located along the spine.

Each chakra governs specific organs, emotions, and life experiences. When a chakra is open and balanced, that area of life flows. When it is blocked — through trauma, stress, suppressed emotions, or negative thought patterns — problems begin to appear.

## The Seven Chakras

### 1. Root Chakra (Muladhara) — Base of Spine

Colour: Red | Element: Earth

This is your foundation. It governs survival, safety, money, home, and family. A blocked root chakra feels like constant financial fear, instability, physical weakness in the legs, or feeling "ungrounded."

### 2. Sacral Chakra (Swadhisthana) — Below Navel

Colour: Orange | Element: Water

This is the seat of creativity, sensuality, and emotions. Blockages here show as creative stagnation, emotional numbness, guilt, reproductive issues, or difficulty experiencing joy.

### 3. Solar Plexus Chakra (Manipura) — Above Navel

Colour: Yellow | Element: Fire

Your personal power lives here — confidence, self-esteem, willpower, and decision-making. A blocked solar plexus creates people-pleasing behaviour, digestive issues, lack of direction, and fear of criticism.

### 4. Heart Chakra (Anahata) — Centre of Chest

Colour: Green | Element: Air

Love, compassion, forgiveness, and connection. This is the bridge between the lower and upper chakras. Heart chakra blockages manifest as difficulty giving or receiving love, holding grudges, loneliness, and heart or lung issues.

### 5. Throat Chakra (Vishuddha) — Throat

Colour: Blue | Element: Ether

Expression, truth, and communication. When blocked, you struggle to speak your truth, feel unheard, fear judgement, or experience thyroid and neck problems.

### 6. Third Eye Chakra (Ajna) — Between Eyebrows

Colour: Indigo | Element: Light

Intuition, wisdom, and clarity. A closed third eye creates confusion, overthinking, poor memory, inability to trust your instincts, and frequent headaches.

### 7. Crown Chakra (Sahasrara) — Top of Head

Colour: Violet/White | Element: Thought

Your connection to the divine, universal consciousness, and higher purpose. Blockages here create a deep sense of meaninglessness, disconnection from spirituality, and chronic exhaustion.
