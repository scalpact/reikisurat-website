## Tired but wired

"I am exhausted, but I cannot sleep." It is one of the most common things we hear from working professionals. The body is tired; the mind keeps running through tomorrow's meetings. Over weeks and months this turns into anxiety, headaches, acidity, back pain and a short temper at home.

This state is often described as the body being stuck in "fight or flight". Rest is not just about time off; the nervous system has to switch into its calm, repair mode.

## How Reiki helps

During Reiki, most people drop into a very deep state of relaxation within 10 to 15 minutes. Breathing slows, shoulders soften and many people fall asleep on the table. Clients regularly tell us they sleep better that night and feel lighter for several days.

Reiki does this gently, without needing you to "do" anything. That is why it suits people who find it hard to sit and meditate.

Reiki supports well-being. If you have severe anxiety, panic attacks or depression, please also see a doctor or counsellor. Reiki works well alongside their care.

## A 10-minute self-calming routine

You do not need to know Reiki to try this. Do it in bed before sleep.

1. Lie on your back and put your phone out of reach.
2. Place both palms on your upper chest. Breathe in for 4 counts, out for 6 counts. Repeat 6 times.
3. Move your hands to your stomach, just below the ribs. Keep the slow breathing for 2 minutes.
4. Place one hand on your forehead and one on the back of your head. Stay for 2 minutes.
5. Let your hands rest by your sides and notice where your body feels heavy.

If you have learnt Reiki Level 1, do the same positions with Reiki flowing.
