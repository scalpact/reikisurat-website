## Purifying the Body's Filters

Kidney health and urinary functions are primarily controlled by the Swadhisthana (Sacral) and Muladhara (Root) chakras. Imbalances in the water element often lead to swelling or urinary incontinence.

Reiki treatment for kidney problems focuses on these two chakras as well as the localized kidney area on the back. This energy flow helps in detoxifying the organs and restoring the balance of elements within the body.
