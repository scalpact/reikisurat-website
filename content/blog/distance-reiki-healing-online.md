## Does Reiki work at a distance?

Yes. Distance healing is part of traditional Usui Reiki and is taught at Level 2. The healer uses specific Reiki symbols to send healing to a person who is not in the same room, whether they are in the next building or another country.

Many people are surprised by how much they feel: warmth, tingling, heaviness in the hands and feet, or simply deep calm. Others notice the results later, in better sleep or a lighter mood.

## What happens in an online session

1. **You book a time.** Online slots at our centres are open every day from 8 AM to 9 PM.
2. **You get a Zoom link** on WhatsApp before the session.
3. **A short talk.** The healer asks what you need help with.
4. **The healing.** You lie down comfortably with your camera on or off. The healer sends Reiki for 30 to 45 minutes. Most people keep their eyes closed and simply rest.
5. **After.** A few minutes to share how you felt, and simple advice for the next days.

## How to prepare

- Choose a quiet room and tell your family not to disturb you for an hour.
- Keep your phone on silent, except for the Zoom call.
- Wear loose, comfortable clothes.
- Keep a glass of water nearby and drink it after the session.
- Do not drive or start heavy work straight after. Give yourself 15 minutes.

## Who distance healing suits

- Busy professionals who cannot spend time in traffic
- Elderly parents and people who find travel difficult
- Family members in another city or abroad
- Anyone who feels more relaxed in their own home

You can also book distance healing **for someone else**, such as a parent in hospital. Share their name and photo with the healer on WhatsApp. Please continue all medical treatment.
