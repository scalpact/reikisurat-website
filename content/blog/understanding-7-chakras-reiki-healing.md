## The Energy Centers Within You

In Reiki practice, we recognize that the physical body is supported by a Prana (Energy) body. Within this body are seven primary energy centers known as Chakras. Each chakra is responsible for different physical, emotional, and spiritual functions. When these centers are blocked, it can lead to disease; when they are open and balanced through Reiki, we experience health and vitality.

## The Seven Main Chakras and Their Functions

1. **Root (Muladhara) Chakra:** Located at the base of the spine. It represents stability and physical strength. Deficiencies here can cause bone disease, weakness, and mental depression.
2. **Sacral (Swadhishthan) Chakra:** Located in the lower abdomen. It is the center for emotions and creativity. Imbalances often manifest as urinary or gynecological issues.
3. **Solar Plexus (Manipur) Chakra:** The center of power and wisdom. A deficiency here is responsible for 60% to 70% of diseases, including diabetes, acidity, and skin disorders.
4. **Heart (Anahat) Chakra:** The center of love. Blocks here can lead to heart and lung problems, as well as feelings of disappointment or low blood pressure.
5. **Throat (Vishuddha) Chakra:** Governs communication. Imbalances may cause thyroid issues or respiratory problems like asthma.
6. **Third Eye (Ajna) Chakra:** The center of intuition and vision. Balancing this chakra through second-degree Reiki enhances telepathy and mental clarity.
7. **Crown (Sahasrara) Chakra:** Located at the top of the head. It connects us to spiritual consciousness and controls the brain parts.

## How Reiki Balances Your Chakras

Through specific hand positions and the use of sacred symbols like 'Sei Hei Ki' (for emotional balance) and 'Cho Ku Rei' (for power), a Reiki practitioner can remove chemicals accumulated by negative thoughts and restore the natural flow of energy. Daily practice and 'Chakra Balancing' exercises can keep these centers vibrant, ensuring long-term health and a positive outlook on life.
