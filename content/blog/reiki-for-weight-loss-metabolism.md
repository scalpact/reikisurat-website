## Balancing the Metabolic Fire

Weight management in Reiki is achieved by harmonizing the glands responsible for metabolism. The treatment involves placing one hand on the Throat chakra (Vishuddha) and the other on the Manipur chakra (Solar Plexus).

This dual-action treatment helps to either reduce or increase body weight by balancing the fire element and metabolic rate. It is a natural way to achieve a shapely body without the side effects often found in chemical supplements.
