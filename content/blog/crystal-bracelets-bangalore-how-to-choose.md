## How to spot fake crystals

Bangalore's markets and online stores are full of dyed glass and plastic beads sold as crystals. Some simple checks:

1. **Too perfect:** natural stones have small variations in colour and pattern. Every bead looking identical is a warning sign.
2. **Very bright colour:** dyed stones are often unnaturally bright, with colour collecting in cracks.
3. **Too light:** most real stones feel cool and slightly heavy in the hand.
4. **Very cheap:** genuine AAA-grade crystal bracelets cannot be sold for a few hundred rupees.
