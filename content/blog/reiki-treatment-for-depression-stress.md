## Healing the Emotional Body

Science suggests that 90% of diseases are psychosomatic, meaning they originate in the mind and thoughts. Prolonged mental stress can lead to chemical accumulations in the body that cause physical illness.

To treat depression and hopelessness, Reiki focus is placed on the Ajna chakra (the seat of intuition) and the Swadhishthana chakra (the emotional center). This combination helps break the cycle of negative thoughts and restores emotional balance.
