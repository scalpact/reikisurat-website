## When the mind will not switch off

Long hours in business, family duties, exams and late nights: many people who come to our Adajan centre say the same thing. "I am tired, but my mind keeps running." Over time this shows up as poor sleep, headaches, acidity, back pain, anger and worry.

The body needs to move from "alert" mode into its calm, repair mode. Rest alone does not always do this.

## How Reiki helps

During a Reiki session most people relax deeply within 10 to 15 minutes. Breathing slows, the shoulders soften and many fall asleep. Clients often tell us they sleep better that night and feel lighter for several days.

Reiki needs no effort from you, which is why it suits people who find meditation hard.

Reiki supports well-being. If you have severe anxiety, panic attacks or depression, please also see a doctor or counsellor; Reiki works well alongside their care.

## A 10-minute routine for tonight

1. Lie on your back with your phone out of reach.
2. Place both palms on your upper chest. Breathe in for 4 counts and out for 6 counts, six times.
3. Move your hands to your stomach, just below the ribs, and keep breathing slowly for 2 minutes.
4. Place one hand on your forehead and one behind your head for 2 minutes.
5. Rest your hands by your sides and notice where the body feels heavy.

If you have learnt Reiki Level 1, do the same positions with Reiki flowing.
