## Energetic Support for Mother and Child

Reiki provides a safe, touch-based method to support women during pregnancy and the post-conception period. It is particularly effective for preventing miscarriage and supporting a normal delivery.

The protocol involves focusing energy on the Swadhishthana (Sacral) and Muladhara (Root) chakras to ensure physical stability, as well as the Ajna and Heart chakras for emotional peace. This holistic approach keeps both the mother and the developing child healthy.
