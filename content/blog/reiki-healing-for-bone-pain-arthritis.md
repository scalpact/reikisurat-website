Physical strength and stability are governed by the Root (Muladhara) chakra, which is directly related to the skeletal system. When this chakra lacks energy, the body becomes stiff, and bone diseases or joint pain can manifest.

Reiki treatment for arthritis and joint problems involves focusing energy on the Ajna chakra and the Muladhara chakra while applying hands directly to the affected part. This process stimulates bone formation and removes the lethargy associated with chronic pain.
