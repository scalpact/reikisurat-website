## Nurturing the Center of Love

The Heart (Anahat) chakra is the center of the circulatory system and our emotions. Deficiencies here can lead to high or low blood pressure and even physical heart disease.

To keep blood pressure normal, Reiki practitioners place one hand on the Heart chakra and the other on the back at the Swadhisthana chakra. This creates an energy circuit that stabilizes the heart's rhythm and calms the nervous system.
