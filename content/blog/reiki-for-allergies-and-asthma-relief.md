## Clearing the Air Pathways

Allergies to dust, smells, and changing seasons often manifest as blockages in our respiratory system. In a Reiki session, we target the 'Tips of the Lungs' and the Heart (Anahat) chakra to clear these pathways.

For chronic conditions like asthma, the focus remains on the Throat (Vishuddha) chakra and the lungs. This helps in reducing inflammation and calming the body's over-reactive response to external triggers.
