2. **Allergies and Respiratory Relief:** To overcome allergies caused by dust, smell, or seasonal changes, treatments focus on clearing the respiratory system. Practitioners often use specific points on the lungs and throat chakra to ease asthma and breathing difficulties.
3. **Skin Diseases and Inflammation:** Whether it is acne, blisters, or more chronic skin conditions, Reiki helps by purifying the Prana (energy) body, which directly impacts the gross physical body.

It is important to remember that Reiki can be safely continued alongside any medical treatment. Never stop your prescribed medication; instead, let Reiki enhance its effectiveness.
