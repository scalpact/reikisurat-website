## Building Your Energetic Shield

The Reiki Kavach is a protection technique used in the third degree to create a luminous shield of white light around the body. This shield protects the practitioner from impurities and negative elements for 24 hours.

To form this shield, you must first purify each of the seven chakras. Once balanced, visualize the Reiki energy spreading from your hands to form a white luminous 'kavach' that seals your energy body against external disturbances.
