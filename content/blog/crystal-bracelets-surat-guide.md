Many bracelets sold in markets and online are dyed glass or plastic. Watch for:

1. **Every bead identical.** Natural stones always vary a little in colour and pattern.
2. **Very bright colour.** Dyed stones often look unnaturally bright, with colour gathered in the cracks.
3. **Light weight.** Real stones feel cool and slightly heavy in the hand.
4. **Very low price.** Genuine AAA-grade bracelets cannot be sold for a few hundred rupees.
