## Who can learn Reiki?

Anyone. You do not need any experience, special belief or "gift". Students at our centres include homemakers, IT professionals, doctors, teachers, chartered accountants and college students. If you can sit quietly and follow simple steps, you can learn Reiki.

## The Usui Reiki course path

We teach traditional Usui Reiki in clear stages. Each level builds on the one before.

### Reiki Level 1, 2 and 3 Certification

The foundation, taught as one programme over about 63 days.

- **Level 1:** self-healing and the hand positions for each chakra. You start practising on yourself from day one.
- **Level 2:** the Reiki symbols and distance healing, so you can send Reiki to family members in another city.
- **Level 3:** deeper practice and a stronger attunement.

You finish with a daily routine you can follow at home in 20 to 30 minutes.

### Reiki Advance Healer

About 21 days, after Level 3. Advanced techniques for specific problems, faster results and protection methods.

### Reiki Master

About 21 days, after Advance Healer. This is the teacher level: the master symbol, how attunements are given, and how to teach.

### Reiki Grand Master

The highest level in our lineage, for Reiki Masters who want to train and attune other masters.

### Two specialisations

- **Crystal Reiki (7 days):** which stone to use for which problem, cleansing and charging crystals, crystal grids.
- **Vedic Numerology:** birth, destiny and name numbers, Lo Shu grid and remedies.

## What is an attunement?

An attunement is the moment the teacher opens your energy channels to Reiki. It is quiet and simple: you sit with eyes closed while the master works. Many students feel warmth or deep calm. In our lineage, attunements are given by a Reiki Grand Master, in person or at a distance.

## Learning in Bengaluru or online

Bengaluru students can learn in person with Reiki Grand Master Dr. Varun Patel, or join online. Online batches suit people with long office hours, and distance attunements work the same way.
