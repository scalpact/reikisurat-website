## Why so many people in Bangalore are trying Reiki

Bangalore runs on long workdays, long commutes and screens. Many people who come to us describe the same things: a mind that will not switch off at night, a stiff neck and back, low energy by mid-week, and a feeling of being stretched thin. Reiki is a gentle, hands-on (or hands-near) healing method from Japan that helps the body settle into deep rest, which is where it repairs itself best.

Reiki is not a replacement for a doctor. It works alongside medical care, and it is safe for all ages.

## What happens in a Reiki session

A Reiki healing session at our Bengaluru branch takes about 60 minutes:

- A short talk about what is troubling you: sleep, stress, pain, relationships, work.
- You lie down fully clothed. The healer places their hands lightly on or just above the main energy centres (chakras).
- Most people feel warmth, tingling or heaviness, and many fall asleep.
- At the end you get simple advice to continue at home.

You can also book the same session **online**, as distance healing on a Zoom call. Many busy professionals in Whitefield, Electronic City, HSR Layout and Hebbal prefer this because there is no traffic involved.

## How to choose a Reiki healer or teacher in Bangalore

Reiki is not regulated in India, so it is worth asking a few questions before you book:

1. **Who is the teacher, and what is their lineage?** Traditional Usui Reiki is passed from master to student. Our Bengaluru branch is led by Reiki Grand Master Dr. Varun Patel with founder Dr. Chirag Gajjar, a Grand Master in the 11th generation of the Usui lineage.
2. **How long have they practised?** Our centre has been healing and teaching since 2014 in Surat, with more than 25 years of Reiki practice behind it.
3. **Are prices clear?** Avoid anyone who pushes expensive packages before you have had a single session.
4. **Do they respect medical care?** A good healer will never ask you to stop your medicine.
